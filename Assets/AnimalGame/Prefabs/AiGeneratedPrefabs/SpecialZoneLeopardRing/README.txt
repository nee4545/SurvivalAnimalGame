SpecialZone Leopard Head Ring (visual-only prefab)

Import the Assets/SpecialZoneLeopardRing folder into your Unity project.

Contents:
- Prefabs/SpecialZone_LeopardHeadRing.prefab
- Materials/SpecialZone_LeopardHeadRing_Mat.mat
- Textures/SpecialZone_LeopardHeadRing.png
- Shaders/SpecialZone_UnlitTransparent.shader

Usage:
- Drag the prefab under your existing trigger zone object.
- No scripts or colliders are included.
- Place slightly above ground (Y 0.03 to 0.06).
- Scale the root object to match your trigger zone radius.
- Glow property name for scripts: _GlowIntensity
