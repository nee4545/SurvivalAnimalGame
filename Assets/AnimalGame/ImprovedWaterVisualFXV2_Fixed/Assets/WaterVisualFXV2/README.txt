Fixed Improved Water Visual FX V2

How to use:
1. Copy Assets/WaterVisualFXV2 into your Unity project.
2. Let Unity compile.
3. Go to Tools -> Water Visual FX -> Create Improved Water FX Prefabs.
4. Generated assets will appear inside the same WaterVisualFXV2 folder, under Generated/Prefabs.
5. Drag WaterVisualTrigger_Stylized into your scene.
6. Scale its BoxCollider to cover your water area.
7. Assign your water plane/mesh Transform to Water Surface Reference.
8. Make sure your player tag is Player.

This fixed version works even if the folder is placed inside another folder.
